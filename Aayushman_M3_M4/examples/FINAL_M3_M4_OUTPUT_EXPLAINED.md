- `risk_score`: 0.0 to 100.0 numerical engineering score calculated by `RiskEngine`.
- `risk_level`: `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`.
- `mitre_mappings`: List of MITRE ATT&CK techniques (`T1046`, `T1498`, etc.).
- `iocs`: Extracted IP and domain indicators enriched with local reputation.
- `attack_chain` & `timeline`: Chronological multi-event entity tracking.
- `explanation`: Qualitative SOC briefing generated by NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning.
